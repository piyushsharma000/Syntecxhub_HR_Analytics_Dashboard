HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

Project Overview

This project is an HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze employee attrition, retention, and workforce trends. The dashboard helps HR teams identify departments, age groups, salary bands, and job roles with higher attrition and supports data-driven HR decision-making.

Key Metrics

- Total Employees: 1,48K
- Attrition Count: 238
- Attrition Rate: 16.1%
- Retention Rate: 83.9%
- Average Employee Age: 36.9 Years

---

Analysis Included

- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Marital Status
- Job Role Satisfaction Analysis
- Attrition by Education Field
- Attrition by Salary Band
- Attrition by Job Role

---

Key Insights

- The Research & Development department has the highest attrition.
- Employees aged 26–35 show the highest attrition count.
- Male employees contribute a larger share of attrition.
- Employees in the Up to 5k salary band have the highest attrition.
- Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition among job roles.

---

Tools & Technologies Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel (dataset)
- Data Visualization

---

Project Workflow

1. Imported HR employee data from Excel.
2. Cleaned and transformed the data in Power Query.
3. Created calculated measures using DAX.
4. Built interactive charts, cards, and slicers.
5. Analyzed attrition patterns and generated insights.
